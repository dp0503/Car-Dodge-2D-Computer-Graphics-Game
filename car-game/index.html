How to play

Move the green car left and right to avoid the other cars. The score goes up while you stay in the game.

←→AD

Graphics concepts used

  1. 2D coordinates: Objects are drawn at x and y positions on the canvas.
  2. Basic shapes: Rectangles and rounded rectangles make the road and cars.
  3. Translation: The drawing origin moves to each car, so its parts can be placed relative to it.
  4. Animation: The game redraws the scene repeatedly while moving the traffic and road markings.
  5. Collision detection: The game checks whether the player car overlaps another car.

In short: The game draws shapes, moves them each frame, and checks for collisions.

Made using HTML Canvas and JavaScript
